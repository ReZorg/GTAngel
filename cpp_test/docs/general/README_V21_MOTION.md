# GTAngel v21 — Deep Tree Echo motion prototype

This is a **clean-room, visual-only** motion iteration on the independently validated GTAngel v20 Win32 game. It translates the user's October 2026 DTE player-motion style guide into an original procedural player silhouette and pose controller. **The reference JPEG/WebP images are pose and material direction, not 3D meshes, skeletal clips, texture atlases, or motion capture.** No video was generated, no source images are projected onto the body, and no likeness or photo-real rendering is claimed.

The archive's `references/` directory includes unchanged copies of the supplied style guide, walk/combat pose boards, and two guard/combat stills for future rigging. The guide also names historical `/home/ubuntu/echo-angel-video/...` anchors that are **not** part of this package; do not treat those paths as resolved 3D assets.

## Try it on the Windows desktop

Run `run_v21.bat` in `C:\ddd\GTAngel\cpp_test` (or start the adjacent `gtangel_agi_v21.exe`). **Space** starts/pauses simulation, **V** switches to third person if needed, **W/A/S/D** retain v20 movement. On foot, **B** starts a visual 36-frame combat entry; after the guard locks, **B** folds the wings through recovery. **I** toggles a small exploration-inspection head overlay. **Q** remains cart boarding; B will not begin while boarded. **E** remains Ariadne and governed beacon interaction; **F5/F9** remain atomic v20 save/load. Combat preview has no attack, damage, casting authority, or independent locomotion.

## Implemented

- 24-tick walk reference cadence linked to **locally measured body speed**, with opposite arm/leg phase, low pelvis bob, planted-foot loop seam and a folded/quiet two-wing profile. The renderer's 50 ms tick makes the authored frame count a *24 fps timing reference* rather than a literal 24-fps display guarantee.
- 36-tick entry: alert/anticipation → mechanical root/boom opening → root-to-tip plate fan → small cyan cosmetic hand orb → held guard. **Exactly two** wing roots attach at the shoulder-back harness. Recovery relatches and fades the orb; no teleporting wings or continuous exploration glow.
- Original low-poly lilac/black/purple outfit cues, aqua crown, amber headset and blue cheek indicators, two titanium-like wing booms and purple/coral plates. All are generated by existing triangle/bone/joint drawing primitives inside the **true 768×768 colour/depth/ObjectAvatar buffer**. There are no imported third-party meshes or textures.
- The animation rig is **output-only**: it reads v20's authoritative body pose/speed and never writes body position, control targets, collision, world versions, beacon state or motor/niche commits. The v20 `SpinalReflex`, `WorldGovernance`, Ariadne quest, and v20 save/load namespace are byte-identical in the generated source. Telemetry remains optional and dormant.

## Evidence and next-quality gates

The native motion harness checks 20 pose/render invariants, including 24-tick cadence, 4–6-tick stop blending, 36-tick transition, fold/recovery, exactly two wing roots, avatar ID/depth evidence, and an unchanged world. The retained v20 CNS adversarial suite checks 124 conditions; the Ariadne native verifier checks all 17. The standalone 768×768 benchmark also verifies the beacon's actual re-observation after WGR repair. `v21_source_verification.json` and `v20_render_benchmark.json` hold reproducible results.

The copied Windows executable passed a **five-second process/window responsiveness smoke test**: title `GTAngel v21 - DTE motion / governed world`, `Responding=True` (process ID 60748 at test time). This is not a screenshot or user-visible playthrough and does not verify actual keyboard or avatar quality on the workstation.

**Still needed for a production-quality likeness:** a licensed/modelled T- or A-pose mesh, UV/material authoring, one shoulder-root hierarchy per wing, skinned hair/skort/ribbon with collision, authored heel/toe keys or IK contact locks, animation retargeting, and filmed Windows third-person acceptance. Motion remains in-place: engine-side root translation is never derived from these pose boards and still belongs exclusively to the v20 body effector. This deliberately follows the [Unity root-motion distinction](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/RootMotion.html) and [Unreal movement-versus-animation boundary](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/root-motion-in-unreal-engine) without importing either engine.

The supplied screenshot and frame stills show the **reference character/video**, not the v20/v21 Win32 app; they cannot be used to certify on-workstation GUI appearance. Verify this build visually in the running Windows window before signing off avatar quality, key handling, or full quest playback.
